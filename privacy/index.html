Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Zodiac Orbit ("the game") is made by Solmatter ("we"). This policy covers the game published on Google Play and on the App Store (com.solmatter.zodiacorbit on both).

In short

  • The first time you open the game, it asks your age, and you start by agreeing to the Terms of Service and this policy. Nothing is sent before you agree.
  • Once you agree, the game sends records of your runs and crash reports to Google Firebase, to help us improve the game. You can switch this off at any time in Settings › About, and the game is the same either way.
  • In the European Economic Area (the European Union and its neighbours), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, records are sent only if you also tick "Send play records" on the start screen.
  • Records of children (under 14 in Korea, under 16 in the European Economic Area, under 13 elsewhere) are never sent. The age you enter is not sent; the device notes only whether it was a child's.
  • The game collects no name, no account, no email address, no contacts, no precise location and no advertising ID.
  • From the second run on you may choose to watch an ad (Google AdMob) for a reward. Ads are shown only when you ask for one, never on a child's device, and never after you have bought the Supporter. The Supporter is paid through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.

What is sent

Play records. They go to Google Analytics for Firebase.

  • When you open the game: how many days since you installed it, whether this is the first time, and your device's model and operating system version (Android or iOS).
  • When a run starts: the wanderer and rune you start with, whether you changed them since your last run, the constellation and difficulty, how many constellations you have relit, your game settings (music and sound volume, vibration, auto, speed, large text, dimmed light, screen shake, damage numbers, power saving), and how loud your phone's media volume is set.
  • When a wave ends: the wave number, the health Polaris has left, and how long the wave took.
  • When you choose a card or a wanderer: what you were offered, what you picked, whether you used a swap, and how long you took.
  • When a run ends, or you leave it: win or loss, the wave you reached, what defeated you, your best hit and totals, how long ready skills waited, your team and cards, and how smoothly the game ran (frames per second).
  • Other moments: taking up a run again, reviving, finishing a hint, unlocking something, and the answer you tap to a one-question survey. The game never sends text you type; it has none.

What Google Analytics adds by itself. A random ID for this installation of the game, the time, the game's version, your device's brand and model, its operating system version, its language, and your approximate location (country, region and city). Google works the location out from your IP address and, according to Google, does not keep the address in Analytics.

Crash reports. If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives the technical trace of the crash, the state of the device at that moment (model, operating system version, free memory and storage), a random installation ID, and where in the game it happened (the stage and the wave).

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, account or contacts.
  • Your precise location.
  • The advertising ID or Android's device ID. On Android the game is built without the permission to read the advertising ID; on iPhone it never asks to track you, so neither the game nor its ads can follow you across other companies' apps. It tells Google Analytics never to use the records for advertising.
  • Photos, files, microphone or camera. The game asks for none of these.
  • The age you enter on the start screen. The device notes only whether it was a child's, and sends it nowhere.

Ads

The game's only ads are the ones you ask for, to get a reward: a revive, twice the Stardust after a win, one more card swap, or the daily gift. They are shown by Google AdMob, from your second run on.

  • What AdMob receives when an ad is asked for or shown: your IP address (from which Google works out your approximate location), your device's model, operating system and language, the game's identity and version, how you interact with the ad, and an identifier that a device gives to all the apps of one maker (Android's app set ID, the identifier for vendors on iPhone). Google uses these to show the ad, to count it, to limit how often the same ad is shown and to prevent fraud.
  • Not personalised: as the game has no advertising ID and never asks iPhone to track you, the ads are not chosen from what you do in other companies' apps.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland Google's consent form asks you first, and you can say no; the rewards are still yours.
  • Never on a child's device (as the age given on the start screen says), and never after you have bought the Supporter.
  • Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The Supporter is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. The store takes your payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card or account details. The store tells the game whether the purchase belongs to your store account, so that it can be restored on a new device. If you allowed play records, Google Analytics also records that a purchase was made (which product, its price and currency).

Why we collect it

Only to improve the game: to see where runs end, which cards and wanderers are chosen, where the game is too hard or too easy, and where it crashes or runs slowly. We do not sell the records, do not use them for advertising and do not build a profile of you.

We rely on your agreement on the start screen (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, on your ticking "Send play records" there). Switching sending off in Settings › About takes it back.

Who receives it

Google (Google LLC and its affiliates) receives the records as our service provider, for two services: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Google handles them on our instructions under its data processing terms. The records are sent encrypted (HTTPS) and may be stored and processed in the United States and in other countries where Google has data centres.

We share the records with no one else.

  • How Google handles data in Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

How long it is kept

  • Google Analytics: records that still point to an installation ID are kept for up to 14 months. Summary figures that no longer point to any installation are kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and their IDs are kept for 90 days, and then removed.
  • Firebase installation ID: a random ID Firebase gives each installation of the game. It points to an installation, not to a person. Google keeps it until we delete it.

If we later keep a copy of the records anywhere else, we will change this policy first.

Your choices

  • Switch sending off: Settings › About › "Send play records". Switching it off stops the sending at once. It also clears what the services still held on your device, and the random ID they knew this installation by.
  • Have your records deleted: while sending is on, Settings › About shows an ID for this installation. Write to the address below with that ID and we will delete the play records sent under it. The records carry no name, account or contact detail, so without the ID we cannot tell which are yours.
  • If you switch sending off first: the ID is discarded, and a new one is made if you switch sending on again. Note the ID before you switch off if you want the earlier records deleted. Otherwise they are removed when the periods above end. Crash reports are removed after 90 days in any case.
  • Questions and requests: write to the address below. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

What stays on your device

The game also writes things on your device that it does not send.

  • A play log (a file named runs.jsonl in the game's own folder). It holds the same records as above for every run, whether or not sending is on.
  • Saved things: your progress, what you have unlocked, your Stardust, your settings, a run you left in the middle, the day you agreed on the start screen and whether the age given was a child's, and whether sending is on.

Android's backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android keeps a copy of the saved things (not the play log) in your own Google account, and puts it back when you install the game again or move to a new phone. Google runs this backup under Google's Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the game does not remove that copy. Google's help page on backup (support.google.com/android/answer/2819582) says how to turn the backup off and where to manage or delete your backups.

iPhone's backup. If iCloud Backup is on, or you back your iPhone up to a computer, the backup holds a copy of the saved things (not the play log). Apple runs iCloud Backup under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it. Deleting the game does not remove that copy. Apple's help page on iCloud Backup (support.apple.com/108770) says how to manage it.

Uninstalling the game deletes the play log and the saved things from the device. On Android, so does Settings › Apps › Zodiac Orbit › Storage › Clear data.

Sharing a result. If you tap the share button on the result screen, the game hands a picture of your result to the app you choose. The game itself sends it nowhere.

Other parties

  • The game contains Google AdMob, for the ads above, and the stores' own billing (Google Play Billing, Apple's StoreKit) through Unity In-App Purchasing, for the Supporter.
  • The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting and diagnostics services are switched off.
  • Google Play delivers the game and keeps its own installation, crash and usage statistics under Google's Privacy Policy. Google shows us those figures through the Play Console. That collection is done by Google Play, not by the game.
  • The App Store does the same on iPhone: Apple keeps its own installation, crash and usage statistics under Apple's Privacy Policy, and shows us those figures in App Store Connect only from people who chose to share them with app developers.

Children

The game is not directed at children. If the age entered on the start screen is a child's (under 14 in Korea, under 16 in the European Economic Area, under 13 elsewhere), no play records or crash reports are sent, Settings › About has no switch to send them, and no ads are shown: the rewards are given without them.

Changes to this policy

If a later version collects anything else, or adds ads, purchases or accounts, we will change this policy, the game's Data safety section on Google Play and its App Privacy details on the App Store before that version is released. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

기간이 끝난 기록은 Google의 시스템에서 지워집니다. 나중에 기록의 사본을 다른 곳에 두게 되면 이 방침을 먼저 고칩니다.

이용자의 선택과 권리

  • 보내기 끄기: 설정 › 정보 › "플레이 기록 보내기". 끄면 바로 보내기를 멈춥니다. 서비스가 기기에 아직 갖고 있던 것과, 이 설치를 가리키던 무작위 ID도 함께 지웁니다.
  • 보낸 기록 지우기: 보내기가 켜져 있는 동안 설정 › 정보에 이 설치의 ID가 보입니다. 그 ID를 적어 아래 주소로 보내 주시면 그 ID로 보낸 플레이 기록을 지웁니다. 기록에는 이름, 계정, 연락처가 없어서 ID 없이는 어느 기록이 누구의 것인지 저희도 알 수 없습니다.
  • 먼저 보내기를 끄면: 그 ID는 버려지고, 다시 켜면 새 ID가 만들어집니다. 앞서 보낸 기록을 지우고 싶다면 끄기 전에 ID를 적어 두십시오. 적어 두지 않은 기록은 위 보관 기간이 끝나면 지워집니다. 오류 기록은 어느 경우에나 90일 뒤에 지워집니다.
  • 문의와 요청: 열람, 정정, 삭제, 처리 정지를 비롯한 문의는 아래 주소로 보내 주십시오. 개인정보 침해에 대한 상담과 신고는 개인정보침해신고센터(privacy.kisa.or.kr, 국번 없이 118)나 개인정보분쟁조정위원회(www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972)에 할 수 있습니다.

자동으로 모으는 장치와 거부 방법

게임에 든 Google Firebase의 프로그램(SDK)이 위 기록을 자동으로 보냅니다. 이 프로그램은 꺼진 채로 들어 있고, 시작 화면에서 동의한 뒤에만 켜집니다. 설정 › 정보에서 언제든 끌 수 있습니다.

기기에 남는 것

게임은 보내지 않는 것도 기기에 적어 둡니다.

  • 플레이 기록 파일 (게임 전용 폴더의 runs.jsonl). 보내기를 켰는지와 관계없이 모든 판에 대해 위와 같은 기록을 담습니다.
  • 저장 내용: 진행 상황, 얻은 것, 별가루, 설정, 중간에 남겨 둔 판, 시작 화면에서 동의한 날과 어린이인지, 보내기를 켰는지.

Android 백업. 휴대폰 설정에서 백업을 켜 두었다면 Android가 저장 내용(플레이 기록 파일은 제외)의 사본을 이용자 본인의 Google 계정에 보관하고, 게임을 다시 설치하거나 새 휴대폰으로 옮길 때 되돌려 놓습니다. 이 백업은 Google이 Google 개인정보처리방침에 따라 운영하며, 저희는 접근할 수 없습니다. 게임을 삭제해도 그 사본은 지워지지 않습니다. 백업을 끄고 관리하는 방법은 Google의 백업 도움말(support.google.com/android/answer/2819582)에 있습니다.

iPhone 백업. iCloud 백업을 켜 두었거나 iPhone을 컴퓨터에 백업하면, 그 백업에 저장 내용(플레이 기록 파일은 제외)의 사본이 들어갑니다. iCloud 백업은 Apple이 Apple 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 운영하며, 저희는 접근할 수 없습니다. 게임을 삭제해도 그 사본은 지워지지 않습니다. 관리하는 방법은 Apple의 iCloud 백업 도움말(support.apple.com/108770)에 있습니다.

게임을 삭제하면 플레이 기록 파일과 저장 내용이 기기에서 지워집니다. Android에서는 설정 › 애플리케이션 › Zodiac Orbit › 저장공간 › 데이터 삭제로도 지울 수 있습니다.

결과 공유. 결과 화면의 공유 버튼을 누르면 게임이 결과 그림을 이용자가 고른 앱에 넘깁니다. 게임이 직접 어디로 보내지는 않습니다.

다른 회사

  • 게임에는 위의 광고를 위한 Google AdMob과, 서포터 결제를 위한 스토어의 결제 기능(Google Play 결제, Apple StoreKit)을 쓰는 Unity In-App Purchasing이 들어 있습니다.
  • 게임은 Unity 엔진으로 만들었습니다. Unity의 분석, 오류 보고, 진단 서비스는 모두 껐습니다.
  • Google Play는 게임을 전달하면서 설치, 오류, 사용 통계를 Google 개인정보처리방침에 따라 따로 관리하고, 그 수치를 Play Console로 저희에게 보여 줍니다. 이것은 게임이 아니라 Google Play가 하는 수집입니다.
  • iPhone에서는 App Store가 같은 일을 합니다. Apple은 설치, 오류, 사용 통계를 Apple 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 따로 관리하고, 앱 개발자와 공유하기로 고른 이용자의 수치만 App Store Connect로 저희에게 보여 줍니다.

안전을 위한 조치

기록은 암호화된 통신으로만 보내고, 기록을 볼 수 있는 Firebase 계정은 저희만 씁니다.

어린이

게임은 어린이를 대상으로 하지 않습니다. 시작 화면에 넣은 나이가 어린이(한국 만 14세 미만, 유럽 경제 지역 만 16세 미만, 그 밖 만 13세 미만)이면 플레이 기록과 오류 기록을 보내지 않고, 설정 › 정보에도 보내기를 켜는 곳이 없으며, 광고도 나오지 않습니다. 보상은 광고 없이 드립니다.

방침의 변경

이후 버전에서 다른 것을 모으거나 광고, 구매, 계정을 넣게 되면, 그 버전을 내기 전에 이 방침과 Google Play의 데이터 보안 항목, App Store의 개인정보 보호 항목을 먼저 고칩니다. 맨 위의 날짜가 이 방침을 마지막으로 고친 날입니다.
